**Description** (478 of 500 characters; the first 250 stand alone)
CURB is a free map of San Francisco street parking rules. Every swept curb is colored by its next street sweep. A typical sign gives a two hour window, but about 77% of tickets are written in the first 45 minutes. On most blocks CURB shows when tickets usually land, based on about 815,000 past citations. Push alerts and calendar reminders before a sweep. Web plus a free iPhone app. No account, no ads, no cookies. Open source. San Francisco only. The posted sign always wins.

## First comment: write this one yourself

Product Hunt's rules say comments must not be written by an AI, and they hide comments that are reported
as generated. So this is a fact sheet, in a sensible order, for you to say in your own words.

1. Who you are: product designer in the Haight, built it alone after too many street sweeping tickets.
2. It covers San Francisco only.
3. The finding: you matched about 815,000 street cleaning citations (2024 to mid 2026) to the blocks they
   were written on. Signs give a two hour window, but about 77% of tickets are written in the first 45
   minutes and about 90% in the first hour. On a typical block most tickets land in the same 20 minutes or so.
4. What it does: every swept curb colored by its next sweep; tap a block for its schedule, the usual ticket
   time on most blocks, and a push alert or calendar reminder before the sweep.
5. What it is not: live. Ticket times come from past citations. The posted sign always wins.
6. Free on web and iPhone, no account, no ads, open source (MIT).
7. Other cities: you are researching Boston and Buenos Aires, no promises. If someone's city publishes its
   citations, you would like to hear about it.
8. What feedback you want: what confuses people on first open, and whether the ticket times read as
   guidance or as a guarantee.
