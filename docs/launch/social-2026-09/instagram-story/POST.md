# Instagram Story · how to post

Format: a 6 frame Story, 1080x1920 (9:16): ig-s01.png to ig-s06.png in this folder. A Story needs no caption. The words live on the frames, and the links live in link stickers (Instagram allows one link sticker per frame).

Keep every sticker between y 250 and 1580, clear of Instagram's top bar and reply bar. Stickers are added in the Instagram app, never baked into the PNG, so they stay tappable.

## How to place them

- Center each sticker in its empty area and scale it to fill most of it.
- Keep every sticker between y 250 and 1580. The top 250px holds the progress bar and profile, the bottom 340px holds the reply bar.
- Instagram allows one link sticker per frame, which is why the two links sit on two frames (3 and 6).
