# LinkedIn · how to post

Format: text post + a 6 page PDF carousel, curb-linkedin-carousel.pdf in this folder (li-01.png to li-06.png are the same pages as images, for review). Slides: my QA team left 1 star, what they got me to ship, alerts that actually arrive, one tap to your block, I hired backup, where it stands. Links go in the first comment, not the post.

## PDF carousel title

My QA team left 1 star. (CURB 1.0.2)

## Post

```
CURB's QA team is one stranger with a 1 star review titled "No longer working."

Harsh. Accurate. Hired.

Here's what they got me to ship, on the web and on iPhone:

→ Alerts that actually arrive. CURB checks every 15 minutes now, and a sweep before 7 AM gets one "move it tonight" alert at 9 PM.
→ Locate finds your block on the first tap. It used to take two.
→ A smoother block sheet.
→ CURB's own map, so no provider change can take it down. $0.
→ A page for every one of SF's 12,253 swept blocks, linked from its neighborhood.

So the QA team gets a break, I also hired backup: CURB now checks itself every 30 minutes, runs a real browser test every night, and emails me if something breaks. Tests went from 92 to 263.

I built the update with Claude Code as my AI pair. I made the calls, it helped me move fast.

Since June: 8,000+ visitors, 380 iPhone downloads, 4.2 stars on the App Store. Hoping the QA team updates theirs.

What's the best 1 star review you've ever gotten?

Free, no ads, no account. Links in the first comment.
```

Before posting:
1. The review is real: the 1 star App Store review you replied to is titled "No longer working." Only the title is quoted; its body is not in the verified facts.
2. Numbers: 8,242 visitors in the last 3 months (Vercel Analytics), 380 App Store downloads Jun 24 to Sep 26, 4.2 stars from 5 ratings, 134 alert sign ups.
3. The Claude Code line stays (owner's call).
4. Attach curb-linkedin-carousel.pdf (6 pages) as a document with the title above.

## Alternative hooks (swap in for the first three lines)

1.
```
Best bug report I got this year: 1 star, titled "No longer working."

No screenshots. No steps to reproduce. Completely right.
```

2.
```
Nothing gets a side project shipped faster than a 1 star review titled "No longer working."

It was not working. Now it is, and it's better.
```

Why the main pick: "Harsh. Accurate. Hired." is the shortest punchline and it matches the cover slide word for word. Hook 1 lands best with engineers. Hook 2 is the broadest, but the softest.
