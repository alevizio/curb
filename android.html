CURB for Android.

CURB for Android is in beta on Google Play, and anyone can join. Three steps:

  1. Join the CURB Android beta group

    It needs a Google account.

    Join the group ↗

    groups.google.com/g/curb-android-beta

  2. Become a tester on Google Play

    Become a tester ↗

    play.google.com/apps/testing/guide.curb.android

  3. Install CURB from Google Play

    Install CURB ↗

    play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=guide.curb.android

It's the same map with the same alerts. Send feedback to hello@curb.guide.
